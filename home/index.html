About Us

Valley Hospital

Valley Hospital is a multi-specialty healthcare provider located in Putalisadak, Kathmandu. We offer quality medical services powered by experienced doctors, modern diagnostics, and a patient-first approach.

Mission

"To provide compassionate, accessible, and high-quality healthcare services through skilled professionals and modern facilities—ensuring every patient receives safe and timely care."

Vision

"To be a trusted and leading multi-specialty hospital in Kathmandu, known for excellence, innovation, and a commitment to community well-being."

Message From the Chairman

"Welcome to Valley Hospital.
Our goal has always been to create a healthcare environment where patients feel safe, respected, and genuinely cared for. We believe that trust is the foundation of good medicine, and every service we provide is built around that principle.
At Valley Hospital, we strive to combine experienced medical professionals, modern facilities, and compassionate care to ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients. As we continue to grow, our focus remains on delivering accessible, affordable, and high-quality healthcare to the people of Kathmandu and beyond.
Thank you for placing your trust in us. We are committed to serving you with dedication, integrity, and excellence."

— Chairman, Valley Hospital Pvt. Ltd.
