About Us

Valley Hospital

Valley Hospital Pvt. Ltd. (Previous, Valley Maternity Nursing home) a renowned General hospital located in heart of Kathmandu City is Serving Nepalese People since 1994 AD. We have a team of young energetic and experienced medical professionals. We are specialized in Obstetrics and Gynecology and many other Medical Speciality like General Medicine, Pediatrics, leprocopy surgery, General Surgery, ENT, Dermatology, Othopedic and others Menopausal & Subfertility Clinics are also running. We have all type of surgical facilities with ICU and NICU Beds. We have 24 Hours Pathology, Pharmacy, X-ray and Ultrasound Service. We cater Immunization Service with availability of vaccines like BCG, HPV, and many othere . We are open every day and all the time. We are trying to fulfill our corporate social responsibility by organizing Free Health Camp at Kathmandu and other remote Places We have Successfully Completed Health Camps in rural village of different parts of the country and in Valley. We have provided Discount Cards for the Patients attending Health camps. They can enjoy happy discount at any Medical service of this Hospital We ensure quality service at reasonable Price. We are always at your service, Give us an opportunity to serve you.

Facilities & Services

Your Health, Our Complete Approach

Obstetrics & Gynecology

Obstetrics & Gynecology focuses on women’s reproductive health, covering pregnancy and childbirth as well as the diagnosis and treatment of female reproductive system disorders.

Pediatric

Pediatrics is the branch of medicine focused on the health, growth, and development of infants, children, and adolescents, including the prevention and treatment of childhood diseases.

General Medicine

General Medicine is a medical specialty focused on the prevention, diagnosis, and non-surgical treatment of a wide range of adult diseases and chronic health conditions.

General Surgery

General Surgery is a medical specialty that focuses on the diagnosis and surgical treatment of a wide range of conditions involving the abdomen, digestive tract, soft tissues, and other organs.

Ear Nose and Throat

Ear, Nose, and Throat (ENT), also called Otorhinolaryngology, is a medical specialty that deals with the diagnosis and treatment of disorders related to the ear, nose, throat, and related structures of the head and neck.

Menopausal Clinic

A Menopausal Clinic specializes in the healthcare of women undergoing menopause, focusing on managing symptoms, hormonal changes, and associated health risks to improve quality of life during and after this transition.

Infertility Clinic

An Infertility Clinic specializes in diagnosing and treating couples or individuals who have difficulty conceiving, offering medical, surgical, and assisted reproductive treatments to help achieve pregnancy.

Pathology

Pathology is the branch of medicine that studies the causes, nature, and effects of diseases by examining tissues, organs, bodily fluids, and autopsies to aid in diagnosis and treatment.

Vaccinations

Vaccinations are medical interventions that stimulate the immune system to protect against specific infectious diseases by introducing weakened or inactive forms of pathogens or their components.

Neuro Medicine

Neuromedicine (or Neurology) is the branch of medicine that focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and management of disorders affecting the nervous system, including the brain, spinal cord, and nerves.

Orthopedics

Orthopedics is the branch of medicine that focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of disorders and injuries of the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and spine.

Dermatology

Dermatology is the branch of medicine that deals with the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of skin, hair, and nail disorders.
